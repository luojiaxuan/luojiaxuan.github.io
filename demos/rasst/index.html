    How the replay works

    Each clip is cut from a full talk that both systems translated end to end during evaluation, at latency multiplier 2 (1.92-second chunks, about two seconds behind the speaker). Text appears at the point in the audio where the system produced it in that run, and the lookup log shows the glossary entries RASST retrieved for each chunk, how long the lookup took, and whether the translation then used the entry. Nothing here runs live.

    How terms are counted

    A term counts as right when the glossary's translation appears in the system's output for the clip, the same exact-form rule as the paper's term accuracy. The counts describe these clips only.
